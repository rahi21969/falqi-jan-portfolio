For My Sister

My Beloved Sister Dida

Didu, I do not know how to express my love for you. You are the best thing that has happened in my life. I have no words to thank Allah for giving me a sister like you.

You do not know how much I love you or how much you mean to me. You were always there for me whenever I needed you, even when no one else was. You are the strongest pillar in my life—the person I can trust easily and for whom I would do anything without thinking twice.

I will always be there for you, Didu, no matter where life takes us. Always remember that you can count on me. In sha Allah, when I am able to earn, I promise I will give you every happiness in this world.

I want you to proudly say: “Yes, he is my brother. When he is with me, I am not afraid of anyone. He is my biggest supporter and caretaker.” Yes, Didu, I will be your caretaker in every universe—the one you can always trust, get angry with, and cry with without worrying that he will judge you.

You prayed for me every time. When I am with you, I feel happiest, safest, bravest, and strongest. When you tell people, “Yes, he is my brother,” nothing else matters to me.

If there is another life, I want to be your brother again and again. You are the best gift in my life: my sister, my supporter, and my beloved Didu. I love you the most.
